We usually file retrofit under "ESG cost." The model says that's the wrong column.

For a real pre-1919 terrace, full fabric retrofit:

- £10,728 outlay
- Paid back in 5.4 years — bill savings alone, nothing else counted
- £60,706 net saved, not spent, over the remaining 30 years of its working life
- Cost per tonne of carbon abated: negative (–£1,694/tCO2e average-grid basis, –£555 to –£635 marginal)

Negative-cost decarbonisation isn't "cheap abatement." It's cash-positive before any carbon value is counted at all — the no-regrets category, not the discount-rate-dependent one.

There's a compliance floor under the upside too: Minimum Energy Efficiency Standards are being extended to social housing for the first time, and a home that misses the deadline is a landlord-specific balance-sheet exposure — not just tenant discomfort. Usefully, the regulation itself caps the downside at £10,000/property.
